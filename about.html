Corporate Profile

Our Identity and Strategy

MESOKURTIC SUPPLY CHAIN LIMITED is a registered Nigerian private limited liability company. The company provides transport, warehousing, redistribution services, inbound logistics solutions, merchandising, and supply chain consulting services.

Our Vision

We are the preferred reference of excellent supply chain service delivery in all our chosen markets.

Our Mission

To provide cost-effective, befitting business solutions and be the vanguard in the provision of these services, which will ensure that the very needs of our customers are continually met.

Core Values

Team spirit, mutual respect, candour, initiative, integrity, competence, innovation, customer service, and a can-do spirit.

Quality Policy

To understand and surpass our clients’ needs and provide responsive and responsible high standards value adding solutions. Our trucks are fully tracked for On Time and In Full delivery.

Fleet and Equipment

Market Position and Strategy

We understand our client’s peak requirements and provide a fleet of thirty-ton to forty-ton vehicles, including Box Body, Flatbeds, and Curtain-sided trucks.

All services are delivered in strict compliance with industry Health, Safety, and Environment guidelines and regulations to protect workers and equipment.
