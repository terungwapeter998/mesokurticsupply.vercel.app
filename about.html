Directors and Management Profile

Mr. Ademeso Olakunle Samuel

Founder / Managing Director

Mr. Ademeso Olakunle Samuel holds a Bachelor’s degree in Physics from the prestigious Obafemi Awolowo University(OAU). He earned a Master’s degree in Business Administration(MBA) with a specialization in Procurement and Supply Chain Management from Ladoke Akintola University of Science and Technology (LAUTECH).

He is a member of the Chartered Institute of Supply Chain Management and the Chartered Institute of Warehousing and Materials Management. He also holds a Diploma in Transport and Logistics Management from the College of Logistics and Supply Chain Management, International Premier Business School.

Mr. Olakunle began his professional career at Guinness Nigeria Plc (Machfinh Ltd.) as a Stock Verifier in 2013, where he and his team managed inventory and supported logistics operations. He later joined Arowolo Oil and Gas as a Manager in 2016, overseeing more than ten (10) fuel stations, before progressing to the role of Business Developer in 2018.

In 2020, he joined Dangote Petroleum Refinery and Petrochemicals as a Supply Chain Specialist, a position he currently holds.

Mrs. Eneji Taiwo Abiola

Executive Director

Mrs. Eneji Taiwo Abiola is a seasoned Procurement and Supply Chain Management professional with over a decade of experience in strategic sourcing, category management, procurement planning, and supply chain operations across manufacturing and service industries.

She holds a Bachelor of Business Administration from Lead City University, Ibadan, and is currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Logistics and Supply Chain Management at Rome Business School.

She also holds an Executive Business Leadership Development Certificate from Rome Business School, a Postgraduate Diploma in Procurement and Supply Chain Management from the College of Logistics & Supply Chain Management, as well as a Professional Diploma in Procurement, Sourcing and Supply Chains.

She is a Chartered Member of the Chartered Institute of Supply Chain Management and an Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Warehousing and Materials Management.

Mrs Taiwo began her professional career with Ohams Realty Limited in 2013 as a Business Development Executive/Administrative Officer. In 2014, she joined Meyer Plc as a Purchasing and Supply Officer, where she later rose to the position of Acting Head, Purchasing and Supply, overseeing procurement operations for manufacturing materials and logistics services.

She later joined Shepherdhill Security Limited in 2021 as Senior Executive, Administration, managing procurement, logistics, and administrative operations.

In 2024, she joined Livestock Feeds Plc (a subsidiary of UAC Nigeria Plc) as Category Manager (Indirect) a position she currently holds, where she manages procurement planning, supplier coordination, spend analysis, cost and inventory optimization supporting continuous manufacturing operations.

Mrs Taiwo has built strong expertise in supplier relationship management, procurement analytics, inventory planning, and cost optimization, contributing to improved operational efficiency and supply chain performance in the organizations she has served.
